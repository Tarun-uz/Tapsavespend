# Tapsavespend
Simple daily expense tracker that runs in your browser
Log an expense in two taps, see where your money goes,
and add it to your phone's home screen like an app.

## Features
- One-tap logging with a keypad and categories
- Credits for refunds
- Spending charts for week, month and year
- Monthly budget and custom cycle start day
- Custom categories and CSV export
- Works offline, data stays on your device

## Use it
Open https://Tarun-uz.github.io/tapsavespend/ in Safari,
tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Privacy
No account, no tracking. Everything is stored in your browser.
